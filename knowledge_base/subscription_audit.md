# Running a subscription audit

## The problem
Subscriptions are small, automatic and easy to forget. Streaming apps, music, cloud storage, fitness memberships and premium app tiers each look cheap alone, but together they often cost more than people guess. Because they renew on their own, they also escape the moment of decision that makes other purchases feel real.

## How to audit
1. List every recurring charge from the last three months of statements, with the amount and renewal date.
2. Multiply each monthly cost by twelve to see the yearly cost.
3. For each one, ask: did I use this in the last month? Would I sign up again today at this price?
4. Cancel anything that fails both questions. Downgrade anything you use lightly to a cheaper tier.

## Habits that help
- Rotate streaming services instead of holding all of them: keep one at a time and switch when you finish a show.
- Share family plans where the service allows it.
- Prefer annual plans only for services you have used steadily for a year.
- Put a calendar reminder two days before free trials end.
- A gym membership only pays off if you go regularly; compare its cost per visit with pay-per-class options.
