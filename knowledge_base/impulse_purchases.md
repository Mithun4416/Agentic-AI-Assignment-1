# Rules for impulse purchases

## Why impulse buys add up
Large one-off purchases, such as electronics, gadgets and fashion during sales, can wipe out a month of savings in a single transaction. Online sales, "only 2 left" banners and one-tap payments are built to shorten the time between wanting and buying.

## Simple rules
- The 48-hour rule: for any non-essential purchase above a set amount, wait two days before buying. Many wants fade.
- The 30-day rule for big items: add it to a wish list and revisit after a month.
- Cost in hours: divide the price by your hourly take-home pay to see how many hours of work it costs.
- One in, one out for clothes and gadgets.
- Unsubscribe from sale emails and remove saved cards from shopping sites.
- Avoid no-cost EMI for wants; it makes a large purchase feel small and locks in future income.

## Planning big purchases instead
If a big purchase is genuinely needed, such as a new laptop for work, plan it: set aside a fixed amount each month into a separate goal until you have the money. Buying from savings is calmer than recovering from a surprise hole in the budget.
