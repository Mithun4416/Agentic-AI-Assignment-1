# The envelope method, digitally

## The idea
The envelope method gives every category a fixed amount of money at the start of the month. In the original version, cash went into paper envelopes labelled groceries, eating out, transport and so on. When an envelope was empty, spending in that category stopped until next month.

## Doing it with UPI and cards
Most spending today is digital, but the principle still works:
- Decide a monthly limit for each flexible category: food and dining, shopping, entertainment, transport.
- Some banks and apps let you create separate sub-accounts or "pots"; move each category's limit there on salary day.
- Otherwise, track category totals weekly against the limits using your statement.
- When a category runs out, either stop or consciously move money from another envelope. Moving money is fine; doing it without noticing is the problem.

## Why it works
It turns a vague goal ("spend less") into a concrete number for each part of life. It also makes trade-offs visible: an expensive dinner this week means fewer deliveries next week. Start with envelopes only for the two or three categories where you overspend most.
