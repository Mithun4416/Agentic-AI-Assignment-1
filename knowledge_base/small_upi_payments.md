# Tracking small UPI payments

## Small payments, big totals
UPI makes paying a few rupees effortless, so small payments happen many times a day: tea, snacks, auto rides, parking, small top-ups. Each one is too small to notice, but dozens of them in a month add up to a real amount.

## How to see them
- Filter your statement for debits below a threshold, for example 300 rupees, and add them up by month.
- Look at the merchants behind them. Often one or two places (a café near work, a snack shop) account for most of the total.
- Compare the count of small payments month by month. A rising count is an early warning even before the total looks big.

## What to do
- Decide which small spends you value and keep them guilt-free. Cut the ones that are pure habit.
- Carry a water bottle and a snack to avoid convenience purchases.
- Use a daily or weekly cap for small spends.
- Review small payments once a week instead of once a month, when the details are still fresh.

The goal is not to stop all small spending, but to make sure it is a choice rather than a leak.
