# Setting category limits

## Why limits help
A monthly limit per category turns budgeting into a simple comparison: spent so far against the limit. It catches overspending in the middle of the month, while there is still time to adjust, rather than after the statement arrives.

## Choosing the numbers
1. Take the average spend per category over the last three months.
2. For needs (rent, groceries, utilities), set the limit at about the average.
3. For wants (food delivery, shopping, entertainment), set the limit somewhat below the average.
4. Check that the limits add up to no more than income minus your savings target.

## Tracking
- Check progress weekly. By the middle of the month you should have spent roughly half of each limit.
- If a category goes over, look at the largest few transactions first; one big item usually explains most of the gap.
- Month-over-month rises above 20% in a category deserve a closer look.

## Adjusting
Limits are not fixed. If a limit is always exceeded for a genuine need, raise it and lower a want instead. Review all limits every three months or after a change in income, rent or city.
