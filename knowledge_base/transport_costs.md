# Cutting transport costs

## Know your pattern
Transport spending in cities is a mix of cab rides, auto-rickshaws, bike taxis, metro and fuel. Cab and auto rides are convenient but usually the most expensive per kilometre, and surge pricing at peak hours can double a fare.

## Ideas that work
- Use the metro or bus for regular commutes and keep cabs for late nights, heavy bags or bad weather.
- A metro smart card or monthly pass is usually cheaper than buying tokens and speeds up entry.
- Bike taxis cost much less than cabs for short solo trips.
- Leave a little earlier to avoid surge pricing on routine trips.
- Share rides with colleagues who live nearby.
- Combine errands into one trip instead of several short rides.
- If you own a vehicle, track fuel, parking and maintenance together to see its real monthly cost.

## Set a limit
Look at the last three months of transport spend and set a monthly cap slightly below the average. Many people find that one or two cab rides a day make up most of their transport cost, so even switching a few of those to the metro makes a visible difference.
