# Budgeting for students and early-career earners

## Start simple
When income is small or irregular (a stipend, an internship, a first salary), a detailed budget can feel pointless. A simple structure is more useful: fixed costs first, then a weekly allowance for everything else.

## Steps
1. List fixed monthly costs: hostel or rent, mess or groceries, phone, travel pass, course fees.
2. Subtract them from monthly income to get the flexible amount.
3. Divide the flexible amount by four for a weekly limit. Track it on the statement each weekend.
4. Save something every month, even a small share of income, before spending the rest.

## Common traps
- Splitting bills with friends over UPI and forgetting who owes whom. Settle splits weekly.
- Many small payments (tea, snacks, auto rides) that each feel too small to count.
- Treating the first salary as permanent spare money and upgrading everything at once.
- Paying for several course platforms or subscriptions and using none of them fully.

## A good first goal
Build one month of expenses as a buffer before taking on any EMIs. It gives freedom to handle a delayed stipend or a sudden expense without borrowing.
